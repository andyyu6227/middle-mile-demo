Closed loop, by design. Data flows up (ingest → validate → health → present); when delay or a failed rule is detected, the Alert & Ticket Engine auto-creates a ticket and assigns it to the dataset's Owner PIC — the only human step is resolving it. Upstream apps select sources down, so they always consume governed, validated data.
